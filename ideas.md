# Salama BBQ — Reference-Led Design Direction

## Ground-Truth Reference

The supplied Cabalar reference establishes the target level of expressive restaurant design: oversized condensed type, deep green paired with warm cream, thick outlined controls, editorial food photography, playful graphic marks, generous breathing room, and a navigation system that feels like a set of tactile signboards. Salama BBQ will carry forward that energetic, craft-forward feel without copying the reference's logo, wording, imagery, or individual layout.

## Chosen Direction: Ember Counter

**Design Movement.** A contemporary take on vintage roadside barbecue signage and direct-flash food editorial photography.

**Core Principles.** The page uses confident scale, unapologetic color blocking, tactile print-like textures, and a visible hierarchy that gets users to the phone number fast. Every section balances a large expressive statement with one useful action.

**Color Philosophy.** A near-black forest green makes the food photography and warm cream surfaces feel rich rather than generic. Fired-brick red signals appetite and immediacy, while ember orange is reserved for moments that need a quick pulse of attention.

**Layout Paradigm.** The site is a vertical sequence of asymmetrical hospitality signboards rather than a centered, repeated-card landing page. It begins with a split hero, rolls into a ribbon and marquee-like menu band, then finishes in a bold location-and-call panel.

**Signature Elements.** Checkerboard deli-paper bands, outlined pill controls, chunky typographic price markers, and a small grill-flame badge appear throughout the page.

**Interaction Philosophy.** The browser should feel as direct as stepping to a counter: navigation scrolls to the relevant station, filters immediately reveal the right food category, and every key decision leads to an actual `tel:` link.

**Animation.** Motion is minimal and lively: a gentle ticker crawl, short hover lifts, button press feedback, and a compact mobile drawer transition. Reduced-motion preferences disable nonessential animation.

**Typography System.** Bebas Neue handles huge uppercase display messages and section labels. Space Grotesk supplies clear utility copy, menu descriptions, and phone numbers. The contrast is deliberately diner-sign bold against contemporary legibility.

**Brand Essence.** Salama BBQ is a fast, flame-kissed neighborhood counter for people who want loud flavor and a simple call-ahead pickup. Its personality is **bold**, **warm**, and **unfussy**.

**Brand Voice.** Headlines are short, hungry, and concrete; calls to action are frank and helpful. Example lines: “Big flavor. One quick call.” and “Your grill order is a ring away.”

**Wordmark & Logo.** The wordmark stacks `SALAMA` above `BBQ` in a narrow sign-painter inspired arrangement, paired with a custom flame-and-grill badge—not a default text treatment.

**Signature Brand Color.** **Charcoal Forest** (`#102D25`) is the ownable color anchoring the entire experience.

## Style Decisions

The full stacked **SALAMA / BBQ** wordmark and flame-grill badge remain visible as one lockup in the header. The flame-grill badge functions as a recurring seal in every main page section. Checkerboard bands, stamped labels, framed counter-sign panels, and subtle print texture are distributed across the page so the experience stays tactile and counter-made rather than drifting toward a generic restaurant template. The phone number is repeated as a readable ordering anchor as visitors move through the page.

The opening is a white, media-first editorial stage: a large rounded food image appears before the primary copy, followed by a separate white information band for the headline, call action, and pickup context. This deliberately follows the structure of the supplied reference rather than using a dark split-screen hero.

The hero uses differential scroll parallax as a signature interaction: the headline/call stack, food image, yellow circular “Hot off the Grill” badge, and flame-grill seal travel at distinct speeds. The text remains inside the dark image overlay for contrast and all motion is removed for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
